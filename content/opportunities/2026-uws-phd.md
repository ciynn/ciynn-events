A fully-funded STFC PhD on pear-shaped atomic nuclei using gamma-ray spectroscopy, based at the Paisley campus, with experiments at JYFL (Finland), Legnaro National Laboratory (Italy) and Argonne National Laboratory (USA). It runs for 3.5 years with fees, a stipend of about £20.8k a year and travel, and starts on 1 February 2027. Applicants need at least an upper-second-class degree in Physics or a related subject; it is open to UK citizens and applicants with pre-settled or settled status.

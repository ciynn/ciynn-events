We are holding a presentation evening at the University of Liverpool, and we are looking for speakers from universities and industry across the North West.

Each speaker gives a 10-minute talk on one part of the region's radiation detection and shielding scene, from detection capabilities and the supply chain to healthcare, the environment and skills. There are ten topics and one slot per topic, given first come, first served.

The best talk wins a place on our summer school, and what is presented on the night will help shape what the summer school covers.

Undergraduates, PGR students and early-career professionals in industry can all take part. See the [event page](../events.html#presentation-evening) for the full list of topics and how to enter.

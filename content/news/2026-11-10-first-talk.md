Our seminar series starts on Tuesday 17 November with Prof Peter Bryant, Director of the Chadwick Institute for Nuclear Innovation.

He will speak on radiological protection and on how professional membership groups support people working in the nuclear and radiation fields. Prof Bryant is Chief Executive of the World Nuclear Transport Institute, a member of the International Commission on Radiological Protection and an adviser to the IAEA.

The talk is in the 2nd Floor Meeting Room of the Oliver Lodge Building, from 15:00 to 17:00, with a short introduction to the network first. Everyone with an interest in anything nuclear is welcome, from any department. [Sign up on the event page](../events.html).

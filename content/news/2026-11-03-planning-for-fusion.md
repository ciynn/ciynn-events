---
title: MPs report on planning for fusion power plants
date: 2026-11-03
summary: The Commons Energy Security and Net Zero Committee has reviewed the government's draft planning rules for fusion energy (EN-8).
---
On 3 November, the House of Commons Energy Security and Net Zero Committee published *Planning for fusion* (HC 571). It is the committee's review of the government's draft National Policy Statement for fusion energy (EN-8), which would set the planning rules for future fusion power plants in the UK.

The committee's main points:

- It is not yet convinced that fusion will contribute to UK energy security within the next decade.
- It questions whether a fusion National Policy Statement is needed at this stage, and says the bigger task is to give each industry more confidence in the planning system.
- It says the draft guidance leaves out important provisions on nuclear non-proliferation for prototype fusion plants.
- It asks the government to explain how the local benefits of fusion projects can count in planning decisions from the start.

The government is due to respond by 3 January 2027.

Fusion is a growing area for UK research and jobs. The government has committed £2.5 billion to it, and the STEP prototype plant is planned at West Burton in Nottinghamshire. How fusion plants are planned and sited will shape where the jobs and research go, including in the North West.

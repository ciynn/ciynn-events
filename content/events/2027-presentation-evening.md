Give a 10-minute talk on one part of the North West's radiation detection and shielding scene. The best talk wins a place on our summer school, and what is presented on the night will help shape what the summer school covers.

Each topic has one slot, given first come, first served:

1. Detection capabilities: what radiation detection the North West can already do
2. Demands in the region: where detection and shielding are needed, and what is missing
3. Companies and supply chain: who designs, makes and uses detectors here
4. Research and development: new detectors and techniques from the region's universities and labs
5. Shielding: materials, design and how shielding is tested
6. Decommissioning and waste: measuring and handling legacy material
7. Healthcare and medicine: detection and shielding in hospitals and proton therapy
8. The environment: monitoring radiation on land, at sea and in the air
9. New nuclear and energy: what new reactors and fusion will need
10. Skills and careers: who the region needs, and how to get there

To enter, email us with your name, where you study or work, and your first and second choice of topic. Entries close on **Friday 29 January 2027**. You can enter on your own or as a small team.

Not presenting? Come along to listen, meet people from across the region and ask questions.

---
title: How to add a member article (not published: files whose names start with _ are skipped)
---
Save the article as content/articles/YYYY-MM-DD-short-name.md, starting with this header:

    ---
    title: The article's title
    date: 2026-12-01
    author: Full name
    role: PhD student, Department of Physics
    summary: One or two sentences for the list page.
    ---

Then write the text in Markdown: a blank line between paragraphs, ## for a heading, - for a list,
**bold**, *italic*, [link text](https://...), and ![what the picture shows](../images/file.jpg) for a
picture saved in content/images/.

Before publishing, check that the author has agreed to it going online, that nothing in it is
confidential, commercially sensitive or export-controlled, and that every picture is ours or used
with permission.
